## Brand & Style

The design system projects the heritage, intellectual rigor, and spiritual depth of SMA A. Wahid Hasyim Tebuireng into an authoritative yet progressive digital ecosystem. It fuses **Modern Institutional Editorial** with subtle **Glassmorphism** and tactile warmth, avoiding both sterile corporate minimalism and outdated ornamental historicism. 

The aesthetic serves a dual audience: prospective and current students (Gen-Z, demanding digital fluidness, micro-interactions, and visual charisma) alongside parents, alumni, and academic scholars (requiring institutional credibility, solemnity, and clarity). The UI evokes feelings of moral grounding, academic distinction, optimism, and contemporary excellence through generous breathing room, structured grid geometry, deep forest tones, and polished warm gold accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of institutional heritage grounded in Islamic tradition, paired with high-clarity modern UI hues:

- **Primary (`#0D281E`)**: Deep Forest Islamic Green. Anchors top navigation active states, primary buttons, footers, and large editorial display headers. Conveys grounding, scholarship, and tradition.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` / `#16A34A`)**: Vibrant Emerald. Used selectively for digital action prompts, active badges, key metrics, and hover micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` / `#E5B842`)**: Warm Institutional Gold. Reserved strictly for honors, accreditation markers, badges of merit, achievement counters, and prestigious accents.
- **Neutral (`#181E1B`)**: Dark Charcoal. Delivers comfortable readability on light backgrounds without the harsh, sterile look of pure `#000000`.
- **Surfaces (`#FBFBF9` & `#F4F3EE`)**: Warm off-white sand tones that soften eye strain, creating a parchment-like academic sophistication.

## Typography

Typography balances architectural discipline with youthful momentum:

- **Headings (Sora)**: Geometric, confident, and distinctively modern. Sora prevents the institution from feeling overly antiquarian, lending technical sharpness to quotes, mission statements, and section milestones.
- **Body & Long-form (Inter)**: Industry standard for neutral, undistorted screen legibility. Ensures dense curriculum texts, admissions guidelines, and student portal details remain effortless to read across all densities.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used for metadata tags, pill badges, and compact navigation to inject human warmth and rounder counters into the structural layout.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid with strict vertical cadence built upon an 8px scale:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column grid, 32px gutters, max-width 1280px centered, 48px to 96px section padding. Content sections balance heavy text columns against generous visual negative space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid, 24px gutters, 32px margins. Multi-column editorial blocks collapse gracefully into 2-column or stacked arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, 16px gutters, 16px edge padding. Sticky components condense down to thumb-friendly bottom or top bars.

Spacing creates breathing space around dense educational data, using wide padding inside cards (`space-xl`) and generous editorial section separation (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of **Tonal Layering**, **Glassmorphism**, and **Forest-Tinted Ambient Shadows**:

- **Glass Surfaces**: Modals, sticky headers, and dynamic toolbars implement `rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` and a crisp `1px` translucent border (`rgba(24, 30, 27, 0.08)`).
- **Shadows**: All drop shadows discard pure black in favor of deep green tinting (`rgba(13, 40, 30, ...)`), making elevation feel organic to the school’s color identity.
  - *Elevation 1 (Cards, Tabs)*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(13, 40, 30, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(13, 40, 30, 0.02)`
  - *Elevation 2 (Interactive Hover, Badges)*: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(13, 40, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(13, 40, 30, 0.03)`
  - *Elevation 3 (Overlays, Floating Action, Modals)*: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(13, 40, 30, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(13, 40, 30, 0.06)`
- **Prestige Glow**: High-achiever cards or accreditation highlights may feature a warm amber-gold specular rim: `0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined balance between approachable geometry and architectural weight:

- **Cards & Containers**: Standard cards use `16px` (`rounded-lg`) to `24px` (`rounded-xl`) corner radiuses, giving editorial modules a soft, tactile presence without feeling toy-like.
- **Pills & Badges**: Small interactive controls, category tags, filters, and status markers leverage full pill geometries (`9999px`) to emphasize touch friendliness and Gen-Z fluidity.
- **Buttons & Form Fields**: Set consistently at `10px` to `12px` corner radiuses to reinforce structural reliability.

## Components

### 1. Sticky Frosted Navbar
- **Height**: 76px on initial load; collapses smoothly to 60px on vertical scroll.
- **Material**: Glassmorphism surface with `surface-glass`, `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`, and an ultra-subtle bottom border `1px solid rgba(24, 30, 27, 0.06)`.
- **Elements**: School emblem left-aligned, centered tab navigation in `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium (with a gold indicator dot under active state), and a high-contrast admission CTA button on the right.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Forest Green (`#0D281E`) background, text `#FBFBF9`, subtle `10px` radius. On hover, smooth elevation to Elevation 2 with a scale transformation of `1.02`.
- **Secondary / Accent**: Vibrant Emerald (`#10B981`) background, white text. For key enrollment actions.
- **Prestige Outline**: Transparent background with a 1.5px gold border (`#D4AF37`), gold text, transitioning to solid gold fill on hover.

### 3. Pill Badges
- **Categories & Tracks**: Tahfidz, Cambridge Curriculum, Science Olympiad, and Pesantren track badges are rendered with full pill radius (`rounded-full`), height `28px`, padding `0 14px`.
- **Style**: Light emerald tint background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) with deep green text (`#0D281E`), uppercase label tracking.

### 4. Cards & Editorial Containers
- **Standard Card**: Background `#F4F3EE`, radius `20px`, 1px border `border-subtle`, 24px padding.
- **Honor & Prestige Card**: Dark background (`#0D281E`), cream text, subtle gold corner motif or border glow (`border-gold`), used for alumni showcases and principal messages.

### 5. Interactive Tabbed Navigation
- Segmented pill track container in `#EAE8E1` with a sliding active white pill card that casts Elevation 1.
- Provides immediate categorization between "Kurikulum Kepesantrenan", "Akademik Nasional", and "Program Internasional".

### 6. Stats Counters
- Numbers rendered in `stat-counter` typography (Sora 48px/800) with a subtle vertical gradient from Deep Forest to Vibrant Emerald, paired with small-caps descriptive labels below.
